# Product Glossary

Canonical terms:
- GMV: use the source-defined Gross Merchandise Value; never silently equate it with every revenue concept.
- Gross Revenue: source-defined gross revenue.
- ROI: source-defined return metric; attribution context must accompany it.
- ROAS: source-defined advertising return metric.
- CVR: conversion rate; formula must be tied to the source grain.
- ATC: add-to-cart metric.
- Organic: source-defined organic attribution.
- Paid: source-defined paid attribution.
- GMV Max: TikTok Product GMV Max attribution context.

Definitions live authoritatively in `02_DATA/METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md`.
