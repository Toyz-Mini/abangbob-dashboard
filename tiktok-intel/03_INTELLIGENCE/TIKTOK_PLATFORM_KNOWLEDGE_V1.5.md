# TikTok Platform Knowledge V1.5

Verified against official TikTok documentation on 2026-08-12.

## Product GMV Max
- Optimizes paid and organic traffic.
- Product GMV Max attribution can include paid and organic orders for promoted products while the campaign is running.
- Product GMV Max ROI and cost per order include paid and organic outcomes and should not be treated as ordinary paid-ad effectiveness metrics.
- TikTok says Product GMV Max ROI should not be compared directly with VSA/PSA ROAS because attribution differs.
- TikTok recommends using Gross Revenue alongside ROI/cost per order when evaluating product/video performance.
- TikTok's current best-practice material recommends keeping an ROI setting for at least three full days rather than changing it frequently.

## Product rules
1. Never label Product GMV Max ROI as paid ROAS.
2. Never compare Product GMV Max ROI directly with VSA/PSA ROAS.
3. Preserve GMV_MAX attribution.
4. Show Gross Revenue in GMV Max analysis.
5. Platform facts provide context; they do not prove causality.
